The concept · 15–39 seconds

Review claims, not lines

Click a node to see what that stage contributes.

One claim can cover many changed lines. Each line links back to its claims and evidence.

We attach requirements, assumptions, proof obligations, tests, and counterexamples to the change. A line can link to several claims, while one claim can cover a whole hunk. A missing check remains unknown, never silently green.

The formal claim interface is a design proposal; the guard check and compile probes already exist.

This is our proposed proof-carrying review layer. Each claim has a precise property, scope, assumptions, checker, reproducible artifact, and status: proved within scope, refuted, tested, or unknown. A counterexample is enough to reject this claim. We do not claim to have formally verified the whole library.
